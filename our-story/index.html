Our mission

Building a better way to get things done

We believe productivity should bring peace, not pressure. That is why we built Melio Tasks.

The problem we saw

Most task apps make you feel more stressed, not less. They bury you in complexity, notifications and features you never asked for. They turn something simple into something complicated.

We were convinced there was another way: helping people organise their lives without the anxiety, and feeling accomplished instead of overwhelmed.

Our answer

Melio Tasks was designed from scratch to be different. We focus on what matters: clearing your mind, ordering your thoughts and reaching your goals calmly.

Every feature, every interaction, every design decision answers one question: does this make you feel more in control? If the answer is no, we do not build it.

What we believe in

The principles behind every decision

Simplicity first

Complexity is easy. Simplicity is work. We do that work so you do not have to.

Quality over quantity

We would rather ship ten features that work beautifully than a hundred that barely work.

Designed around you

Every decision starts with the people using the app. Your feedback shapes the product.

Never finished

There is always a better way, and we are always looking for it.

Privacy matters

Your tasks are yours. We do not sell your data and we do not share your information.

Details on purpose

The small things count. Every animation, colour and interaction is deliberate.

Our journey

From an idea to an app in your pocket

2025 Q1

The idea

After years of task apps that added stress instead of removing it, we decided to build something better, something that puts your peace of mind first.

2025 Q2

Building and testing

Months of testing with early users, iterating on the design and refining every interaction. We learned what works and what does not.

2025 Q3

Beta launch

We opened the app to a small group of beta testers. Their feedback shaped the product into what it is today.

2025 Q4

Public launch

After months of refinement, Melio Tasks launched publicly. Users told us the same thing: less stress, more finished.

2026 and beyond

Melio AI, and what comes next

The assistant now writes your plans for you, in five languages. New features and platforms are on the way. We are only getting started.

Join us on the journey

Be part of a community that values calm, progress and peace of mind.
