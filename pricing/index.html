Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before subscribing

Is there a free trial? ↓
Yes. Three free days, once, on either plan. A payment method is required to start the trial, and you can cancel any time before it ends without being charged.
What is included in Melio AI? ↓
Ten messages a day. Each one can produce a complete project with its tasks, its dates and its habits. Nothing is added to your app until you tap Create, and the counter resets every day.
Which payment methods do you accept? ↓
All major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, handled through the App Store and Google Play.
How do I cancel? ↓
From your App Store or Google Play subscription settings, any time. You keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for.
